# Reflection

## The Hardest Concept: Databases

Databases were the most difficult part of this course for me. Designing tables, understanding relationships, and writing queries that actually did what I intended felt abstract at first, and there were moments I felt completely stuck. What helped was patience. I slowed down, rebuilt small examples from scratch, and allowed myself to get things wrong until the logic finally clicked. What once felt confusing now feels like a foundation I can build on.

## What I Would Improve in My Capstone

Based on the feedback I received, I would build up the architecture and secure the backend. My capstone works, but I've learned that working is not the same as well built. I want to organize it more cleanly, separate concerns properly, and protect the backend with stronger authentication, input validation, and careful handling of data. The feedback was uncomfortable to hear at times, but it showed me exactly where I need to grow, and I'm grateful for that.

## What I Will Learn Next

I want to go deeper into AI and how it is used in full stack software engineering, from building AI-powered features to using AI tools to work smarter and faster. I'm excited, and a little humbled, by how much there is still to learn. This course taught me that real progress comes from patience and honest reflection, and I plan to carry both into everything I build next.

## A Vote of Thanks

My sincere thanks to the brilliant team at the Power Learn Project (PLP). Your guidance, patience, and belief in us made this journey possible. Thank you for the feedback that challenged me and for creating a space where learning felt achievable. I am leaving stronger, more confident, and truly grateful.
